## Aluminum extrusion standing desk

This year's splurge was a few hundred linear inches of aluminum extrusion. The frame supports an Ikea tabletop from the dumpster and is bolted on top of a pair of beefy electric standing desk legs:

![desk](/images/things2026/desk.jpeg)

(tabletop not pictured). This aluminum extrusion frame has proved especially useful for bike maintenance, hanging stuff, and arm-wrestling training.

## Neon picture frame

In addition to good coffee, [Work in Progress cafe in Costa Mesa, CA](https://share.google/aEPfKDhHEKlDHQb9S) sells prints. My best friend Cesar mentioned that he'd bought one of these prints, but didn't have the big ~`25x19`" neon acrylic frame that accompanies it:

![reference frame](/images/things2026/reference.jpeg)

So I on the flight to NYC I CAD'd it up. I know a little bit about fabricating with acrylic plastic, so I tried to replicate the original feel as closely as possible out of `1/4"` neon green acrylic. The backing and mat are made of birch plywood, and the mat/poster snap to the frame with little neodymium magnets.

The big challenge here was being limited to a ~`24x12`" nominal workspace on an Epilog laser cutter, while the frame itself is `25x19`". Since the frame dimensions exceed the workspace, I included some dovetails/joinery to assemble the backing and mat. Total part dimensions fit with just enough margin on the Epilog:
